# In the news

Word is getting out about our program. Below are some ways we’ve made the news.

## September 2026

A quick update:
- Volunteers are looking for additional neighborhoods to take advantage of our grant funding within Orange County. The difficulty is finding leaders living within a neighborhood to act as an advocate and lead in organizing the food waste drop-off site for their neighborhood. These leaders would help with designating a suitable drop-off site, distributing any counter-top bins being offered to the neighborhood, or other advocacy efforts.
- Volunteers are working on creating a common yard sign design to help better distinguish a community food waste drop-off site. This would allow neighborhoods who are establishing a new food waste drop-off site to better signal to neighbors that this is a community food waste drop-off.
- The composting guide for Barnes Street was updated with tips on making your food waste collection more manageable. [View the guide at https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZWfRwHzmuTaG8apbZi38lIZMGB0U6n-bq0gZSLYnoQU/edit?usp=sharing](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZWfRwHzmuTaG8apbZi38lIZMGB0U6n-bq0gZSLYnoQU/edit?usp=sharing).
